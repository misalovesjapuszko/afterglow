Afterglow

Your music, in afterglow.

Download Join the Discord

Free for Windows, Linux, iPhone and iPad. Needs Spotify Premium.

Lyrics

Every word, right on time.

Spotify’s own synced lyrics light up word by word.

Lines drift in on soft springs, the current word fills as it’s sung, and the rest blur gently out of focus. If Spotify has no lyrics for a song, Afterglow finds them on LRCLIB.

Album-art colors.

The whole app wears the song.

Liquid glass.

Light, layered and alive.

iPhone and iPad

Made for your pocket.

Native Liquid Glass on iOS 26 and later.

On iPhone the tab bar and mini player are real iOS Liquid Glass, drawn by the system. Afterglow is the remote and the lyrics screen; the Spotify app plays the music.

Everything you play. Nothing in the way.

Smooth transitions

Covers turn away like cards when you skip, in the direction you skipped.

Your whole library

Home, search, liked songs, playlists, albums, artists and your queue.

Plugins and themes

A few lines of JavaScript or CSS add buttons, pages, lyric sources or a new look.

Tiny and quick

About 8 MB. Uses your system’s web engine instead of shipping a browser.

Keyboard first

Space to play, arrows to seek, L for lyrics, Ctrl+K to search.

Sound ring

On Linux, a ring around the cover moves with the music you hear.

Playing in a minute.

No accounts to make, nothing to set up.

Install

Download the file for your device and open it.

Sign in with Spotify

Log in on Spotify’s own page. You need Spotify Premium.

Press play

Open Now Playing and watch the lyrics glow along.
